# Code of conduct

Ferrite contributors must provide a respectful, harassment-free environment for
everyone, regardless of gender identity or expression, sex characteristics,
sexual orientation, disability, neurotype, race, ethnicity, nationality,
religion, age, body size, experience level, or other protected status.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate
misgendering, sexualized attention, doxxing, intimidation, and sustained
disruption of project work.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. The maintainer
may remove comments, restrict participation, reject contributions, or ban a
participant when needed to protect the project and its contributors.
